# Pasyar Marinduque

## Project concept

**Sector:** Tourism & Cultural Innovation  
**Local challenge:** Information about Marinduque's heritage and visitor experiences can be scattered or out of date, making it harder for residents and first-time visitors to discover places and plan a considerate visit. This is a prototype problem statement, not a result of a field survey.  
**Target users:** Marinduque residents, first-time visitors, families, and people interested in local heritage.  
**Proposed solution:** A small, responsive guide that brings a few example experiences together and lets visitors collect them into a simple day plan.

## What the prototype does

- Search and filter a demonstration catalog of heritage, culture, and coastal experiences.
- Add and remove places from an on-page visit plan.
- Share short, practical reminders about respectful visits and checking current local guidance.

The examples are for demonstration. The site does not provide live schedules, transport, availability, safety, or travel-time information. Check current details with local tourism offices and authorized providers before traveling.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a current browser. No installation, backend, database, external API, or network connection is required.
